Support

Before you write

  • Setting up or moving your server: see the self-hosting guide in the project's documentation.
  • What is protected and what is not: see the threat model.
  • Lost a device: in the app, Settings → If You Lose a Device.

Contact

Write to support@noisestorage.com . In the app, Settings → Copy Diagnostic Info, and paste it into your message: it holds versions, states and counts only.

Never send your 24 words, a connect string, a pairing text or an enrollment code. Nobody from the project will ever ask for them.
